Welcome to Lalala Festival

Your festival journey
starts here

LaLaLa Fest is Indonesia’s first international forest music festival, bringing together artists from around the world in a dreamy natural setting. Known for its breathtaking atmosphere under the stars, it’s more than just a concert—it’s a celebration of music, nature, and togetherness. Each year, thousands of music lovers gather among the trees to sing, dance, and escape from the ordinary.

Beyond the music, LaLaLa Fest embraces creativity and self-expression through art installations, immersive experiences, and eco-friendly values. It’s a place where every moment feels alive—where sound, light, and nature blend into one unforgettable vibe. Whether you come for the beats, the scenery, or the energy, LaLaLa Fest promises a weekend that feels like magic.
